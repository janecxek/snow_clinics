Why people choose us

Four reasons people travel for this.

None of them is a machine or a product name. They are the things a clinic has to decide, and mostly decides the other way.

Meet Dr. Snow →

  1. One physician, every treatment

    01

    The person who assesses you is the person who treats you. Nothing is handed to a nurse or an aesthetician after the consultation.

  2. Under-dosed on purpose

    02

    We place less than your face can take, look again at two weeks, and add only what the result asks for. Overfilling is fast to do and slow to undo.

  3. A written plan before you commit

    03

    Sequence, intervals and the full cost, in writing, after the consultation. Nothing is injected on a first visit.

  4. Four clinics, one standard

    04

    Zürich, Konstanz, Paris and Marbella, on a fixed rotation. Same hands, same protocol, in English, German, Spanish or Polish.

Straight answers

The things people ask once the door is closed.

Most people describe injectables as uncomfortable rather than painful. Topical anaesthetic goes on as standard and is left for twenty minutes; fillers contain lidocaine; thread lifting is done under local anaesthetic. You can ask to stop at any point.

Neither do we — it is what this practice is built to avoid. Every plan starts deliberately under-dosed and is reviewed at two weeks. If what you are asking for would change your features rather than restore them, Dr. Snow will say so and offer something smaller, or decline.

Botulinum toxin and mesotherapy: nothing beyond an hour of redness. Lips: two to three days of swelling. Threads: three to five days of tenderness and possible bruising. You are told which week to keep clear before you choose a date.
