The approach

We treat in millimetres.

Overfilling is easy to do and slow to undo. Every plan here starts under-dosed on purpose: we place less than the face can take, review it at two weeks, and add only what the result asks for. It takes one more appointment. It is the difference between looking rested and looking treated.

Anatomy decides the plan, not a menu. Dr. Snow assesses the face at rest and in movement, maps the vectors that have shifted, and treats the cause of the change rather than the line it left behind.

Results

The test is whether anyone notices the treatment.

Photographs are taken at the same distance, in the same light, at every visit. They are how we decide whether to add, hold or stop — and the only honest way to judge a result four weeks later.

Outcomes differ from person to person. Anatomy, skin quality, age and lifestyle all change what is achievable, and every plan is agreed in consultation before anything is booked.

How it works

Four appointments, in order.

Nothing is injected on a first visit. The sequence exists so that you can change your mind at any point before it matters.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Forty-five minutes. Medical history, facial assessment at rest and in movement, photographs, and a frank conversation about what treatment can and cannot do for you.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A written plan with sequence, intervals and total cost, sent to you before you commit. If a treatment is not right for your anatomy, it is not on the plan.

  3. 03

    Treatment

    Performed by Dr. Snow, never delegated. Topical anaesthetic as standard, CE-marked products only, and every batch number recorded in your file.

  4. 04

    Review

    A follow-up at two weeks, included. This is where a result is finished — small corrections now are the reason the outcome looks unremarkable later.
